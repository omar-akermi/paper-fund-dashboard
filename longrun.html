How this team invests

A second paper fund with its own $500 (started 23 Nov 2026) that holds for weeks or months instead of hours. Its money sits in SPY (the S&P 500) by default, so being idle never leaves it far behind the market. Each night after the close the Scout screens today's S&P 500 for stocks in an uptrend with strong 12-month gains, and the Analyst (Claude) may move up to 5 slices of 10% into single stocks it has a reason to expect to beat SPY. Every pick is scored as its return minus SPY's over the same days, so a pick that only matches the market counts as a failure. Sales are automatic when a pick closes under its 200-day average or falls 15% below its entry. Orders go in once a session after 09:45 ET. Week by week this will track SPY closely: telling whether the picks add anything will take many months.
